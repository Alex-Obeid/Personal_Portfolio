Alex ObeidDesign Language · Rev 1.0

Ten
Rules

  1. 01Three colours, no more. Cream, ink, vermillion.
  2. 02Colour arrives in full-bleed panels, never scattered accents.
  3. 03No depth. No shadows, gradients, blur, radius or glass.
  4. 04Hover inverts colour. That is the whole interaction vocabulary.
  5. 05Type is huge or tiny. Nothing comfortable in between.
  6. 06Three faces: Archivo Black, Archivo, DM Mono.
  7. 07Labels are uppercase mono at .2em tracking. Always.
  8. 08Hairlines, not borders. A rule divides; it does not contain.
  9. 09Everything sits on a grid and may bleed off the edge.
  10. 10Asymmetry over centring. Centre only a numeral or a mark.
